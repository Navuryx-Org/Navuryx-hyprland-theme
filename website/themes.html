Visual system

Dark by default. Consistent everywhere.

Navuryx uses restrained black surfaces, royal-blue interaction states and purple accents across the shell, launcher, lock screen and supported applications.

Navuryx Default

Balanced black surfaces with clear royal-blue controls and purple secondary accents.

Royal Glass

Transparent panels and sharper blue borders for users who prefer visible wallpaper depth.

Midnight Purple

Deeper purple surfaces with blue retained for selected and actionable elements.

Abyss Black

A low-distraction near-black option with reduced glow and compact visual emphasis.

Shared theme state

One change updates the desktop.

Theme selection is designed to update Hyprland, Quickshell, Waybar, Rofi, lock and logout surfaces, GTK, terminals and generated application templates together.

  • Wallpaper DynamicDerives colors from the active wallpaper and harmonizes them toward the Navuryx palette.
  • Density controlsCompact and spacious UI spacing.
  • Motion controlsAnimation profiles and reduced-motion support.
  • Surface controlsBlur, transparency, borders, shadows and corner radius.
